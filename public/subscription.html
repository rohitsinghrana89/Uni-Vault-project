Frequently Asked Questions

🔄 Can I switch or cancel plans anytime?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription anytime directly from your Account Settings with zero cancellation fees.

🎁 How does the 14-day free trial work?

All Premium and Ultimate memberships start with a 14-day risk-free trial. You can explore our catalog in 4K before your first billing date.

💳 What payment methods are supported?

We support all major payment options across India and globally: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and digital wallets.

📺 What hardware supports 4K and Dolby Atmos?

4K Ultra HD and Dolby Atmos playback are supported on 4K Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony), Apple TV 4K, Fire TV 4K, Android TVs, and compatible sound systems.
